# Satori neuron (browser app): what changed, for agents

The history of what changed for agents and for people driving the page,
newest first. The current reference is /llms.txt; read that first. An entry's
"below" or "above" meant /llms.txt as it was then.

## Changed 2026-10-28 (Satori Service)

- The Satori Network is called the **Satori Service** in every text (short: "Service": Home's chip, the Models tab's
  tags and filter, the Streams tab's "Service Streams"). Calls and fields keep their names (`central`, `network`).

## Changed 2026-10-28 (the Wallet tab's cards)

- The Wallet tab is a card each: Base (balances, Send), Receive, Claimable, Staking, Preload gas, Details (folded).
  Claim's and Stake's passphrase prompts are in their cards: `data-unlock-for` `wallet-claim`, `wallet-stake`.

## Changed 2026-10-27 (the next step)

- Home's card `next-step` (advice, from the third day the app was opened): the first thing missing for the app to
  predict on the Satori contract by itself: Base ETH in the wallet (Open Wallet: the Wallet tab's Receive, its QR
  code), Preload gas (before Stake: it needs gas there), or Turn on Automatic. `dismiss('next-step')`: Not now, back after 3 days.
- The Wallet tab: `base-receive` shows the wallet's address (`base-receive-address`) and its QR code (`base-qr`).

## Changed 2026-10-27 (How Does This Work?)

- `how` opens with "What is Satori?" and ends with what to do each day: open the app, or play the game.

## Changed 2026-10-27 (the reminder's own time)

- The daily reminder comes at the time of day it was turned on, not 9:00; `reminder().minute` says it.

## Changed 2026-10-27 (the daily reminder)

- `setReminder(on)` and `reminder()`: the daily push (Settings › This device: `reminder`, `reminder-off`, `reminder-on`,
  `reminder-error`). On, the service worker sends the day's batch with the app closed, or reminds.

## Changed 2026-10-27 (the Game's streak)

- The Game tab: `game-balance` moved above the star; `game-streak` (`data-above`) above the value. `gameState()`'s
  streams carry `streak`: `{ streak, bar }` (bar: the game's turning point), or null.

## Changed 2026-10-27 (the Game's one Sent screen)

- After Submit, the Sent screen has `game-tomorrow` and `game-dismiss` at once; `game-done` is gone.

## Changed 2026-10-27 (Preload gas)

- `preloadGas({ amount?, passphrase })`: Base ETH from the wallet to the alias; `base().alias.preload`, its default.
  Wallet tab: `base-preloaded` (the alias's ETH), `base-preload` (fills the Send form with it).
- `contract()` by hand: the `stake` check comes after `gas`.

## Changed 2026-10-27 (by hand: the stake check)

- `contract()` by hand: a `stake` check after `gas`: 'ok' when staked with this app, else 'wait' with `fix: 'stake'`
  (Stake with this app, `contract-stake`, on the card).

## Changed 2026-10-27 (no earlier alias)

- `backup('retired')` is gone, and so are Settings › Backup's `retired-key` and `retired-alias`: an earlier record's
  keys are replaced at its next Unlock and not kept.

## Changed 2026-10-27 (Relay Streams: Predict; no call changed)

- Streams › Relay Streams: each stream's one button (`catalog-action`, on the right) is Predict (follow and predict,
  as `subscribe(pubkey, stream, { predict: true })`) or Stop predicting (`aria-pressed="true"`; unfollow, as
  `unsubscribe`). No Subscribe button there any more; a follow alone is still `subscribe()`.

## Changed 2026-10-26 (derived keys: setup, importDocker, backup, status)

- The alias and the Nostr key are derived from the wallet (docs/design/wallets.md#derived-keys). `setup()` takes no
  `aliasWords` or `nostrKey`; `importDocker()` takes the wallet file only (no `alias`, no `nostr`); Import asks for the
  wallet alone. `status().keysFrom`: 'wallet' | 'signature' | 'random'. `backup('retired', p)`: an earlier alias.
- An earlier record's alias and Nostr key are replaced by derived ones at the wallet's next Unlock (the page reloads);
  the wallet then shows as staked elsewhere until Stake.
- Settings › Backup: Wallet words, Alias key (`alias-key`), Nostr key (`nostr-key`), and an earlier alias's
  (`retired-key`, `retired-alias`); `keys-note` says whether they are derived or random.
- Stored, the alias and the Nostr key are sealed with this browser's own key where it keeps one (no call changed).

## Changed 2026-10-26 (the Streams and Market pages; no call changed)

- Streams: every card folded until tapped, in this order: Network Streams (the Satori Network's line, Details and
  Refresh, was the Satori Network card's Poll now, above its streams), Relay Streams (was Streams on the relays),

  (the same stake as the Wallet tab's) when not staked and the wallet holds SATORI; its ETH line no longer says it.

  is gone.
- Home's title: Predicted at rest (sky), Predicting while it works; `health().title` likewise.
- Wallet tab, Wallets: tapping a wallet's address copies it (`wallet-address`).
- Game tab, after playing: "N Predictions submitted", "They will be scored in 24 hours." (no transaction line). Its value above the chart is the latest value, Sentiment
  streams too (it was their up/down/flat).
- Models: the median model line and the backtest note are gone (`engine-median`, `engine-note`); the Network,
  Contract and Relays toggles show no counts.
- Home: the day strip's key line ("Top: the Satori Network · Bottom: the Satori contract") is gone.

- Market: Selling (each predicted stream: Sell, its buyers, Make free), Buying (Stop, Take back) and Find streams

## Changed 2026-10-21 (paid streams: one price, a daily settle)

- The seller settles at most once a day (it was every hourly pass, and at

## Changed 2026-10-21 (paid streams)

- A priced `_pred` is announced `encrypted: true` with `metadata.market`;

- Streams: Sell and Bought under Satori relays; streams sold on the Satori

## Changed 2026-10-20 (the track record's review: fixes)

- A prediction counts only if it arrived here before its outcome arrived
  here (both on this device's clock), as well as before the outcome's
  timestamp, and within the first half of the interval after its baseline
  arrived here; one prediction per outcome (the earliest received that
  qualifies), so `scored` counts outcomes; a pair whose error is not a
  finite number is not scored.
- `records()` / `record()`: `source.name` is the source's own
  announcement's name, else its stream id (it was the predictor's words
  when the source had none); new `source.announced` (the source has an
  announcement of its own) and `sameAuthor` (the predictor's key is the
  source's author).
- Streams › Prediction streams is grouped by source (`record-source`
  groups with `record-source-name`, `record-source-by`,
  `record-source-note`); a row shows the predictor (`record-by`, and
  `record-own`: "predicts its own stream"); `record-name` is gone; the
  detail says its source (`record-source-line`).
- The cap of 200 prediction streams holds for the whole watched set, not
  per scan: the ones already watched and still announced stay ahead of
  newcomers (`nostr().watching.left`: found by the last scan and not
  watched). A tombstoned `_pred` leaves at once, and so does our own when
  we unsubscribe.
- An observation stamped more than max(its cadence, 300 s) ahead of its
  arrival is not kept (nor forecast, for a subscription), and a watched
  stream's observation without a numeric timestamp is not kept.
- `nostr().relays[]` has `refused` (the subscriptions the relay closed:
  `[{ id, message }]`, a follow chunk it would not take) and `notice` (its
  last NOTICE, or null).

## Changed 2026-10-20 (the track record: how a buyer knows who's good)

- The neuron now watches every prediction stream on the relays on its own
  (a `<name>_pred` naming its source; up to 200, its own first, then the
  newest announced) and each one's source, and keeps a verified track
  record for each: its predictions against no change, counting only those
  this neuron received before the outcome. Watching only reads.
- `await satori.records()` → every watched prediction stream's record,
  best first; `await satori.record(pubkey, stream)` → one, with its last 28
  scored `pairs`. Free to call (Safety).
- `nostr().watching`: `{ streams, sources, found, left, at, error }`.
- `health().work` may be `{ task: 'watch', step: 'ask' | 'verify' }` (the
  scan, shown on Streams, not Home's hero).
- `health().channels.relays` says "Watching 112" when nothing is
  subscribed but prediction streams are watched (it said "No streams",
  off); with every relay down, "Relays down" (warn) on the chip alone.

## Changed 2026-10-17 (latest: advice; the hero stays green)

  warn or bad reason is present, and it may give its channel's chip its

  `state: 'advice'`).

## Changed 2026-10-17 (later: what it is doing; three greens)

- `health().work`: what the neuron is doing now, or null when idle:
  `{ task, step, done, total, since, critical, text }`. `task`: `central`
  (steps `register`, `observations`, `history` with done/total streams,
  `models` with done/total targets, `send` with total predictions), `base`
  (`check`, `send`, `confirm`), `delegate` / `undelegate` / `send` (`send`,
  `confirm` with k of n, `settle`), `claim` (`rewards`, `drop`, `airdrop`,
  `confirm`), `keys` (`save`), `discover` (`ask`, relays answered), `subscribe`
  (`find`). `since`: when the step began (ms). `critical: true`: closing Satori
  now would lose work (sending predictions, a Base batch or transaction not yet
  confirmed, the keys being written); its `text` begins "Keep Satori open · ".
  `text`: the words Home shows ("Loading history · 34 of 82 streams"). The
  entry that leads is shown: a critical one first, else central, base, the
  wallet's actions, keys, discover, subscribe.
- `health().tone`: the colour Home shows, alongside `status`: `'working'`
  (regular green: working as expected; starting and catching up are working),
  `'critical'` (vivid green: closing would lose work), `'idle'` (sky green:
  all good, waiting for the next cycle), `'warn'`, `'bad'` (they win), `'off'`
  (grey: only a channel deliberately not used; never the hero). Each
  `health().channels[x]` has a `tone` too (`working` while its channel has
  work in progress; `off`: Base not delegated here, or no stream subscribed).
- Home's hero (`data-test=health`): `data-tone`, `data-work` (the step it
  shows, `central:history`) and `aria-busy="true"` while working, `data-from`

  short arc turns around its ring while working, the only animation on the
  page (none with reduced motion). Chips have `data-tone`; the action card
  has `data-tone` (it had `data-s`).
- A new neuron's first poll took 17–19 s on central2 (82 streams); now about
  8 s: the poll's writes are a few set statements, central's history is
  fetched 8 at a time and written in a few commits.
